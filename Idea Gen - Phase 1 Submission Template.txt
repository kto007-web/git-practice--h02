Phase 1 - Idea Generation


Idea 1: Medication dispenser
What? A device that detects when medication needs to be given and sets out an alert to it being dispensed.
Why? People with bad memory can forget to take medication so this helps remind them.


Idea 2: Automatic pancake/waffle cooker
What? A pancake cooker that has a timer and a waffle attachment. It also has a cooling system that works fast.
Why? It turns off automatically so it won't burn and the cooling system cools it down fast to prevent injury.


Idea 3: Orange peeling device
What? It peels oranges for people.
Why? Some people hate peeling oranges to this can peel it for them.


Idea 4: Automatic watering pot
What? A pot that detects how moist the soil is in itself and adds water as needed depending on the plant.
Why? Lots of people don't know how much water a plant needs nor if they need to put water in it or not.


Idea 5: Calorie measurer
What? A device that measures how much calories are in a protion of food.
Why? Helps with dieting or weight gaining.


Idea 6: Lost and Found
What? A website that shows thing people lost and if they had been found.
Why? So people have a higher chance of finding what they need.


Idea 7: Debugger
What? A website that scans code, finds errors, and explains how and why the error happened and how to fix it.
Why? It would be a great learning website for beginner programmers fixing their code.
